## Claim and paper reference
Paper: approach.tex eq:programme (share programme Gamma_s(p), maximiser y*), thm:universal (ceiling B_p Gamma_s), cyclic.tex thm:cyclic / prop:block (interference-free class optimum gamma_IF = nu^{-(nu-q)/q} B_p Gamma_s attained by nu-blocks). That trained (interfering) product networks follow y* is a MEASUREMENT, not a theorem (appendix.tex app:remarks Scope (ii)).
Claim under test: product networks trained under the frozen recipe at q < nu allocate amplitude across frequencies as y*(s, p) (modulo dilation): count K = K*(s, p), budget shares w = y*^{1/s}; gamma <= B_p Gamma_s(p) always (theorem: a violation is a bug).
E1b claim: cells with the same s but different (nu, q) behave the same (Gamma_s depends on (nu, q) only through s).

## Convergence criterion and exclusion rules (declared)
- Criterion: core/train.py DEFAULT_CONVERGENCE, applied at the final step: grad_rel = ||g_CE + lam g_R|| / (||g_CE|| + lam ||g_R||) <= 1e-3; relative change of the float64 regularised objective <= 1e-4 and of gamma <= 1e-3 over the final 10% of steps; margin > 0; all values finite.
- Status per run: ok / not_converged / diverged / crashed (RUNS.csv). PRIMARY analysis = status ok. Not-converged runs with margin > 0 are analysed SEPARATELY and reported in the same tables (never dropped). Diverged/crashed runs are listed and counted, not scored.
- No other exclusion.

## Controls
- Positive control: constructed networks (core/construct.py) with the predicted y* at every scored (p, s) (nu-block product networks; for power nu = 3, q = 3 the Morwani construction) are measured and scored by the SAME pipeline; they must score as perfect matches (count exact under the integer definitions, TV ~ 0, cosine ~ 1, gamma/class-optimum = 1 to 1e-6).
- Null baselines: (a) the random-init networks (the step-0 weights of each run's seed, re-generated by core.models.init_params) scored by the same pipeline; (b) a random allocation: y drawn uniformly on the budget simplex with the predicted support size K* on a uniformly random support (1000 draws per cell), scored against y*.
