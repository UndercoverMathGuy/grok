## Verdicts

### R1: CONFIRMED

I rebuilt the proof line by line. (1) Companion Lemma 3: a single feature has g(x,delta)>0 for every delta iff Re Phi > |Im Phi| cot(pi/p). Positive margin is therefore an open condition. Perturbing zero input entries keeps slot symmetry, the outputs and the width. (2) d=(p-1)/2 is nonzero mod p, so d'=d/2 is nonzero and a=b+d' differs from b. Also rho^{2d'}=rho^d=e^{i(pi-theta)}. With N(k)=(n-2k)e_b+2k e_a: Phi(N(k)) = e^{i(pi-theta)k} sum_i C_i r_i^k, where C_i=(2/p)what_i(1)(u_i(b)rho^b)^n and r_i=(u_i(a)/u_i(b))^2>0. Squaring removes every sign, so the sign pattern plays no role. (3) Arc geometry. I_{k+1}=I_k-pi+theta. An arc of length 2theta<pi that avoids the line lands on the other side after rotation by -pi. A further +theta rotation cannot carry it across the line, because that would take a rotation of at least 2theta. For undetermined k (k = k0 mod p), I_{k-1} and I_{k+1} touch the same ray from opposite sides. Undetermined k are spaced p>=3 apart, so consecutive determined values alternate. (4) Laguerre: h_psi is a real exponential sum with at most m distinct positive bases and real coefficients. Unless it vanishes identically it has at most m-1 real zeros. It cannot vanish identically, because D is non-empty. The |D|-1 sign changes give zeros in disjoint open intervals. Choosing k0 in the least populated residue class of {0..K} gives |D|=K+1-floor((K+1)/p). Nothing uses p>=5, b=2 or class C, so the result holds for every odd p, including p=3. Checks (my own code, v_R1.py and v_R1b.py; field computed from the logit table by definition, 50 digits): the identity holds to rel. err 1e-49..1e-51. Alternation was tested at every b and k0 on many networks, with 0 violations: the exact p=3 counterexamples of exact_certificate.py (n=11, 13); the certified 1-D networks p3_n11, n14, n18; the Note's p=5 n=4,6 and p=7 n=3 networks; and 12 positive-margin perturbed IF blocks with 4-7 distinct sign patterns among neurons (p=3,5,7,11; n up to 9). Every p=3 counterexample satisfies the bound (width 8>=4 at n=11, 13>=7 at n=18), so nothing about the p=3 value is implied. Caveat on strength only: the bound is about n/2, far below the observed nu-floor(nu/p), so it does not touch (Q1).

### R2: CONFIRMED

(a) Class C: Phi-A is a sum over non-empty T of b_T z^T, with z_t=rho^{2x_t}; since p is odd, z_t runs over mu_p. A k-fold product has per-slot exponents m_t in [0,k]. E z^m is non-zero iff every m_t = 0 mod p. For k<=p-1 that forces m=0, which is impossible because every T is non-empty. (b) With DC, the slot exponents are in {0,1,2}, so sums lie in [0,2k], inside [0,p-1] for k<=(p-1)/2. (c) Binomial expansion with (a)/(b) gives E(Phi-c)^k=(A-c)^k for k<=(p-1)/2. Pointwise, Re(Phi-c)^k >= cos(k pi/p)|Phi-c|^k because k pi/p<pi/2; then take expectations. Hidden-assumption check: (c) needs only Phi in c+K, and that holds for EVERY network with c=min cm. So it applies with no positive-margin assumption, and I tested it that way. Checks (v_R2R3.py; networks built from weights, Phi from logits): the moments vanish to 1e-16 exactly up to the predicted order (p-1 for class C, (p-1)/2 for DC) and are 1e-2..1e-5 at the next order (p=3,5,7,11). The class-C field is supported on {0,1}^n (off-support coefficients 5e-16). Worst R2(c) ratio was 0.929 over 280 random class-C nets and 0.968 over 300 DC nets. Trivial wording issue: at p=3, part (b) holds only for k=1, where it is trivially true; the report calls this case 'empty'. There is no p=3 value implication.

### R3: MINOR_GAP

The main class-C statement is fully reconstructed and correct. P is multi-affine, so product measures on mu_p give P on conv(mu_p)^n. That polygon product contains the polydisc of radius R=cos(pi/p), so every slice f maps the unit disc into the closed convex sector c+K. Here the half-angle is pi/p<=pi/3, so the sector is convex at p=3 too. For c'<c, G(w)=c'+M(w)^{2/p} with M(w)=(beta+conj(beta)w)/(1-w) is univalent onto c'+int K and has G(0)=A. Rogosinski (Duren Thm 6.4: f subordinate to a convex univalent F gives |a_k|<=|F'(0)|) applies within its hypotheses. Computing the derivative: |G'(0)|=(4/p)|A-c'|cos((p/2)arg(A-c')) <= (4/p)|A-c'|. The edge case A=c forces Phi to be constant, which is consistent. The bound holds for any real c with Phi in c+K, so for EVERY class-C single feature with c=min cm. The pointwise and slot-symmetric corollaries are correct (using |A-c|<=Re(A-c)/cos(pi/p)). Checks (v_R2R3.py): 280 random nets with coefficients obtained by Fourier analysis of the logit-table field give max ratio 0.700 (0.719 against the sharper cos-factor bound). Adversarial Nelder-Mead+Powell maximisation over atoms, beta and zeta (p=3,5,7; n<=3; all S and k) gives max 0.772, below 1. Minor gaps: (i) The DC bullet 'the same holds with radius r_p' is imprecise. The slice degree is |e|_1 with e_t in {0,1,2}, so DC and sign-flip coefficients mix. After zeta-averaging, a sign-flip coefficient beta_T sits at degree 2|T|, so the pointwise bound is r_p^{2|T|}|beta_T|<=(4/p)|A-c|, not r_p^{|T|}. (ii) The radii come from a non-rigorous grid LP. I derived r_5=sqrt(3/8)=0.612372 exactly: the 5 moment points form a simplex, and its barycentric weights give the constraint 3/4-2r^2>=0. My LP (v_rp.py) gives r_7~0.80133 and r_11~0.91895. The quoted 0.612 and 0.801 are safe, but the principle section's r_11=0.919 is rounded up. (iii) 'sqrt(C(n,k)) times stronger than L^2' ignores the cos^k(pi/p) and p factors; it is a heuristic.

### R7c: SERIOUS_GAP

The conclusion does not follow from the stated hypothesis, and its p=7 sentence is false. (1) Applying Thm 14 needs an UPPER bound k*(2s-1,s)<k_p. The value k*(7,4)=0.928 is a local-search LOWER bound (a sup over atoms that were found), and no upper bound exists. This is an unstated second hypothesis. My independent searches (slot-symmetric m=3..12, general atoms m=3..8, about 460 L-BFGS starts; v_R7c.py) reproduce 0.92768 and find nothing larger. That is consistent with the claim, but unproved, and the margin to k_5=0.94574 is only 0.018. (2) For non-half-integer s, 2s-1 is not an integer, so the hypothesis is ill-posed. 'Whenever s<=4' needs k*(ceil(2s-1),s)<k_5 for every s in (1,4], but only half-integer s (b=2) were computed, and k*(n,s) is not monotone in s a priori (nu^{1-s}Q_s^s = nu (m/nu)^s M_{1/s} can move either way when m>nu). (3) The p=7 sentence 'the same holds for s up to about 15' is refuted by certified computation. Arc family plus analytic-gradient polish, re-evaluated in 260-bit intervals (v_R7c_arc.py, v_R7c_polish.py, certificates polished_n28.npy and polished_n29.npy), gives k*(28,14.5)>=1.826174>k_7=1.819874 and k*(29,15)>=1.836287 (arc start alone already 1.82229 at n=29). So even granting the hypothesis, Thm 14 provably cannot cover s=14.5 or s=15 at p=7, and the true threshold is below 14.5. The p=5 statement remains a conjecture conditional on two unproved inputs.

### R8a: MINOR_GAP

The refutation is valid. For beta_i=A/n and xi_it=e^{2 pi i i/n}, the moments are m_k=[n|k]A for 0<=k<=n, so P=A(1+z_1...z_n). I checked this exactly with sympy for n=2..6 and numerically (1e-15) for n=8, 12, 20. The gain is A(1-(n/nu)^{s-1}) because Q^s=n^{s-1}A. The sup of |P-A| over D_R^n is A R^n, so C>=(1-(n/nu)^{s-1})R^{-n}. The numbers 4.55, 86.5, 2.39e4, 1.63e9, 7.13e18 (b=2, R=1/2) are reproduced. Seed S4's tests have |z_t|=eps_t/2<=1/2, so R=1/2 is the right radius, and no n-uniform constant exists. Flaws: (i) The sentence 'at R=1/2 that is >=0.28*2^n, for every s>1' is false. Examples: n=8, s=1.5 gives 0.057*2^n; s=1.05 gives 0.006*2^n; at b=2, n=2 and n=3 give 0.18 and 0.25. It is true at b=2 for n>=4. For fixed s>1 the constant is still >= roughly ((s-1)/nu)2^n, which is unbounded, so the conclusion survives. (ii) Scope: S4 never states a payment, and R8a's formalisation is over ALL atom systems. That is the form of the programme's P12/c_1 payments, and c_12=1-(2/3)^{s-1} is exactly the n=2 member of this family. The family's field is far outside the cone (min cm = -0.618A at p=5, -1.247A at p=7). So a cone-restricted (positive-margin) polydisc payment is not refuted by it.
