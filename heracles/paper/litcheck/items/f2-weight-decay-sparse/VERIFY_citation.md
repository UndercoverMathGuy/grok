## Issues
1. "consistently" is contradicted by McCracken's Fig 4 and Table 5: a one-hot 1-hidden-layer MLP is dense under explicit L2. The L2 there is tiny (1e-5, against wd 1-2 in Nanda, Chughtai, Zhong and Stander), so the dense counterexample sits at small weight decay. That is still "ordinary weight decay".
2. Gromov has NO regularisation in its main setup, so it cannot support a claim about weight-decayed dense nets. In the checker's replacement sentence, "One-hot one-hidden-layer MLPs use all floor(p/2) \citep{gromov,mccracken}", add "with little or no regularization", or cite Gromov only for the no-regularisation case.
3. "moves ... as the degree of the network changes": McCracken attributes the move to depth or a trainable embedding, not to degree. The text must present the degree reading as the authors' own interpretation.
4. The sentence appears twice (intro.tex:6 and prior.tex:9), so any fix must be applied to both. zhong2023clock and stander2024grokking are not in refs.bib yet.
